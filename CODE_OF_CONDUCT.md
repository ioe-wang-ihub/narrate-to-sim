# Narrate-to-Sim Code of Conduct

These project-authored expectations apply to collaboration on the source-only
Narrate-to-Sim client and its documentation, including repository issues,
pull requests and evaluation discussions. They do not claim adoption of an
external conduct standard or institutional policy.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and welcoming across backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss code, evidence and ideas without harassment, discrimination,
  threats, personal attacks or unwanted contact.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Do not share personal details, participant
  data, credentials or private correspondence without permission.
- Give credit and preserve attribution. Report limitations honestly; do not
  present automated diagnostics as completed human acceptance.
- Accept constructive review and follow reasonable maintainer moderation
  requests.

## Concerns and Moderation

Raise concerns in a [public repository issue](https://github.com/ioe-wang-ihub/narrate-to-sim/issues).
For a sensitive report, open a content-free issue asking for a private contact
method instead of describing the matter there.
Do not post personal details, allegations or sensitive evidence in a shared
issue or publicly. No dedicated private inbox or confidentiality guarantee
is offered here.

Repository administrators may request edits, remove inappropriate content, close or lock
discussions, or restrict participation within their repository permissions.
Responses depend on the circumstances; this document does not establish a
separate enforcement team, guaranteed response time or institutional process.
