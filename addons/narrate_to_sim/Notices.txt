SCOPE

This directory contains the lab-authored client, reused authoring UI/parser,
scenario template, tests, launch scripts and documentation. Original SCANER
and DReyeVR authoring and runner filenames remain unchanged. Only the
documented stock-client entry point is supported.

Dependencies below are downloaded separately by setup; their implementations
are not vendored in this source tree. Copies of upstream notices are provided
for reference under third_party_licenses/ and retain their original terms.
Changing this client's license does not change those licenses.
